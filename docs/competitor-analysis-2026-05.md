# Competitor Analysis: website-context
## May 2026

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The AI chatbot market is $13.28B in 2026 (growing 29.5% YoY). website-context occupies a unique position globally — no product combines navigation replacement + flow recording + guided execution + auto-scraped RAG. Poland is the ideal beachhead: 2.21M SMEs, 3.7% AI adoption, EUR 34B EU funding incoming, government targeting 50% AI adoption.

---

## UNIQUE POSITION (NO COMPETITOR HAS ALL FOUR)

1. **Navigation replacement** — chat IS the website, not a support bubble
2. **Flow recording** — owner clicks through a process, AI learns it
3. **Guided execution** — AI fills forms in the user's browser with visual highlighting
4. **Auto-scraped RAG context** — point at a URL, everything builds automatically

## DIRECT COMPETITORS

### SiteGPT
- **Pricing**: $39-$259/month
- **Features**: Website scraping, auto-sync, human escalation, multi-language, lead capture
- **vs website-context**: No flow recording, no guided execution, support widget only

### Chatbase
- **Pricing**: Free / $40 / $150 / $500
- **Features**: 15+ AI models, file upload, lead capture, 15-min deployment
- **vs website-context**: No agentic capabilities, no form filling, no navigation replacement

### CustomGPT
- **Pricing**: $99 / $499 / Enterprise
- **Features**: Anti-hallucination with citations, 1400+ doc formats, 100+ integrations
- **vs website-context**: Enterprise-focused, no flow recording, no guided execution

### Botpress
- **Pricing**: Free / $89 / $495 / $2000+
- **Features**: Visual flow builder, autonomous engine, knowledge bases, RBAC
- **vs website-context**: Flow DESIGN (not recording), developer-focused, no guided execution

### Dante AI
- **Pricing**: Free / $29 / $99 / $299
- **Features**: Voice agents, 7000+ integrations, WhatsApp, 100+ languages
- **vs website-context**: Voice-first, no browser automation, no flow recording

### ChatNode
- **Pricing**: Free / $35 / $89 / $377
- **Features**: AI Actions (Stripe, Calendly), auto-retrain, white-label
- **vs website-context**: Pre-built actions only, no custom flow recording

### Chaindesk
- **Pricing**: $99 / $499
- **Features**: Omnichannel, auto-retraining, human handoff
- **vs website-context**: No guided execution, no flow recording

### DocsBot
- **Pricing**: $49 / $149 / $499
- **Features**: Documentation-focused, SOC 2, analytics
- **vs website-context**: Docs-only, no website scraping, no actions

### Kommunicate
- **Pricing**: $40 / $80 / Enterprise
- **Features**: Multi-model, multi-channel, no-code flow builder
- **vs website-context**: No auto-scraping, no guided execution

## ADJACENT COMPETITORS

### Intercom Fin
- **Pricing**: $0.99/resolved conversation + seat fees
- **Features**: 67% resolution rate, Procedures, Voice, CX Score
- **vs website-context**: Manual KB setup, support widget only, expensive

### Drift (Salesloft)
- **Status**: SUNSETTING March 2026 — replaced by 1mind

- **Opportunity**: Market gap website-context fills at 1/50th the price

### Zendesk AI
- **Pricing**: $19-$115/agent + $50 AI add-on
- **vs website-context**: Enterprise-only, complex pricing, human-agent centered

### Tidio (POLISH — Szczecin)
- **Revenue**: $48.4M (2024), 174 employees
- **Funding**: $26.8M (Series B)
- **Pricing**: Free / $29 / $59 / $749 + Lyro AI $39-289
- **vs website-context**: Support widget, not navigation replacement. Manual FAQ upload, no flow recording, no guided execution. BUT: established Polish brand, 300K+ businesses.

### LiveChat / Text SA (POLISH — Wroclaw)
- **Revenue**: $91M TTM, publicly traded (TXT.WA)
- **Market Cap**: ~$254M
- **vs website-context**: Human-first philosophy. 20+ years in market. Support-focused, not agentic. No auto-scraping, no flow recording.
